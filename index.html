Privacy policy — Home Assistant Nest (personal use)

This Google Cloud app is used only by Nick Cox to connect his own Google Nest devices to his own Home Assistant server at home.

It accesses Nest device data (camera, doorbell and event information) solely to display and control those devices on that server. No data is collected by, sent to or shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party connections.
